Kostas Papazoglou: “Difficult Moments Are What Help You Grow Most as a Coach”

An English-language overview of the conversation about the move to the German BBL, the first season as a head coach at Basketball Löwen Braunschweig, player development and the lessons drawn from a demanding year.

Interview originally published by the Hellenic Basketball League (ESAKE) · In Greek · English overview published on konstantinospapazoglou.com

In the official Man to Man powered by Stoiximan podcast of the Stoiximan GBL, Kostas Papazoglou reflects on his coaching career abroad and his first season as head coach of Basketball Löwen Braunschweig. The conversation looks at differences between Greek and German basketball, professional club structures and the development of young players.

Player development and coaching culture

Papazoglou discusses Germany’s player-development pathway, from youth competitions and development teams to opportunities for young players to work within senior-team environments. He also highlights collaborative coaching, clear organisational roles and evidence-based decision-making as important elements of a professional club.

The Braunschweig season

Reflecting on the season, Papazoglou says that serious and repeated injuries prevented the team from showing its full potential. The young roster initially responded by giving significant responsibilities to teenage players. Further absences among those young players later made it increasingly difficult to maintain the same level over a long period, requiring continued adaptation.

Lessons for the future

One of the central lessons he identifies is the importance of alignment between a head coach and club management, particularly when a team faces difficult circumstances. He stresses the value of establishing shared expectations early, communicating about contingency plans and preparing together for challenging periods during a season.
